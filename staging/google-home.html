Google Home light-pattern system

Role
Lead / Systems Designer
Goal
A coherent light-behavior language that signals device state across the whole Google Home fleet
Team
Product, engineering, hardware

The coordinated light behaviors that tell you what a device is doing. One behavioral language across 25M+ devices. Red Dot award.

Status

Case study in progress. The full write-up lands after the site redesign.

01 / ChallengeThe problem

State legibility across many devices: how a room full of different Google Home hardware can each say what it's doing, clearly and consistently.

02 / SystemDesigning a behavioral language

A shared vocabulary of light behaviors: Listening, Thinking, Confirmed, Muted, readable at a glance and the same everywhere.

03 / ScaleOne system across the fleet

Translating one behavioral language onto very different hardware, from LED rings to bars, without losing meaning.

04 / ImpactImpact

Adoption across 25M+ devices and the Red Dot recognition that followed.

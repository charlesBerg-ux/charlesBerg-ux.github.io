The same bar I sell, held on my own site.

Accessibility is the work I do, so it would be strange to cut corners here. This page is a plain account of what I build to, how I check it, and what to do if something still gets in your way.

What I build to

I build this site to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. In practice that means text keeps a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1, every control is reachable and operable by keyboard, focus is always visible, structure is conveyed with real headings and landmarks, and nothing depends on color alone. The layout reflows without a horizontal scroll down to a 320-pixel viewport and at 200 percent zoom.

Motion is kept minimal, and anything that moves respects your reduced-motion setting. There is no tracking and there are no cookies, so nothing here works against a screen reader or a slow connection.

How I test it

I do not trust a single tool to catch everything, so I use three passes that check different things.

  • KeyboardA manual pass with the mouse put away. I tab through every page to confirm the order is logical, focus stays visible, the mobile menu closes on Escape and returns focus, and nothing traps.
  • Screen readerA manual screen-reader sweep with VoiceOver and NVDA, listening for correct headings, labels, link text, image descriptions, and live-region announcements.
  • AutomatedAn automated axe-core run against every page, targeting zero critical or serious issues. It catches the mechanical failures so the manual passes can focus on judgment.

Automated checks catch perhaps a third of what matters. The other two thirds come from actually using the page the way a disabled visitor would, which is the same conviction that runs through my client work.

Found a barrier? Tell me.

If any part of this site got in your way, I want to know, and I will treat it as a real bug. Email me at charles@crb.is with the page, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology or browser you were using if you know it. I read every message personally and usually reply within two business days.

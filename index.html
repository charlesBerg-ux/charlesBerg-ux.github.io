About Me

I've spent my career at places like Google, Microsoft, and Motorola learning one thing: the best technology disappears. It doesn't ask you to be smarter or faster. It just works.

Now I apply that belief where it matters most — designing for older adults and people with disabilities. Not as an afterthought or a compliance checkbox, but as the starting point. Because when you design for the people tech has historically left behind, you end up building something better for everyone.

I specialize in the intersection of AgeTech, accessibility, and systems thinking — turning complex platforms into experiences that feel obvious, respectful, and empowering.

About

Twenty years of shipping at scale, now applied where it counts.

I spent two decades at Google, Microsoft, and Motorola at the Staff IC level — designing things like the Google Home light system, seen on 25 million devices and other popular features such as Family Wi-Fi on the Nest router line. Somewhere along the way I noticed that most of the products I worked on were great if you happened to be the median user, and quietly hostile if you weren't.

Now I've changed my focus to designing products that work for all types of people, like the Pixel Magnifier. Working through CPACC certification this year. Member of IAAP.
